# Also the height (its field and handle) must survive turning and lifting.
# Turning an object: a 40 x 20 box turned a quarter turn is 20 x 40 in the world, and back; the Rotate
# handle can be dragged round it (rotation about the upright line through its middle).
action NEW_DOCUMENT
wait 800
button Box
wait 800
tree Box
field Width 40
wait 600
expect-extent X 40
expect-extent Y 20
rotate-object 90
expect-extent X 20
expect-extent Y 40
expect-extent Z 20
handles-count 7
rotate-object -90
expect-extent X 40
expect-extent Y 20
handles-count 7
quit
